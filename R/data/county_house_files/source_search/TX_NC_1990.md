# Texas and North Carolina U.S. House by county, 1990 general: source search

Searched 2026-09-24. **North Carolina: solved.** A free official source prints every county in every district, and all printed totals match FEC. **Texas: no better free copy found.** The SRI microfiche remains the only free copy of the official county-by-district report. Below are the ways to confirm or replace it.

---

## NORTH CAROLINA 1990

### Lead 1 (best, verified): *North Carolina Manual 1991-1992*, "General Elections, 1986-1990", pp. 930-933

- Publisher: NC Secretary of State. It is the official state register, and these results are the State Board of Elections abstracts of votes.
- Internet Archive: `https://archive.org/details/northcarolinaman19911992nort`
  The House table is on book pages 930-933, which are IA leaves n954-n957.
  Page image URL pattern: `https://archive.org/download/northcarolinaman19911992nort/northcarolinaman19911992nort_jp2.zip/northcarolinaman19911992nort_jp2%2Fnorthcarolinaman19911992nort_0954.jp2` (0954-0957).
- Same volume at NC Digital Collections: `https://digital.ncdcr.gov/Documents/Detail/north-carolina-manual-1991-1992/4384573`
- Contents: one table per congressional district (1st-11th). Each row is a county, with split counties marked "(Part)", e.g. Johnston (Part) in CD2 and CD3, Moore (Part), Avery (Part). The columns are Nov 6 1990 (D) and (R), then 1988, then 1986.
- The print is clean typeset at about 2100x3400 px. It is far easier to read than any microfiche. The IA djvu OCR scrambles the column order, so transcribe from the images, not the OCR.
- Verification: I checked the printed 1990 district totals against `R/data/fec_official/fec1990_house.csv`. CD1, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 and 11 match FEC exactly for both candidates. CD2 D+R = 130,979+44,263 = 175,242, which equals the FEC district total, so there are no other candidates. The same holds for CD1 (163,358), CD3 (141,535) and CD11 (201,309). Only D and R columns are printed; the FEC totals show there were no minor-party votes to omit. CD8, CD9 and CD10 appear on p. 932 and were not individually re-checked.
- Access: free, public domain (state publication).

Downloaded to `R/data/county_house_files/north_carolina/leads/`:
- `nc_manual_1991_p930_house_1986_1990.png`: CD1, CD2, CD3
- `nc_manual_1991_p931_house_1986_1990.png`: CD4, CD5, CD6, CD7
- `nc_manual_1991_p932_house_1986_1990.png`: CD8, CD9, CD10
- `nc_manual_1991_p933_house_1986_1990.png`: CD11
- `nc_manual_1991_1992_djvu.txt`: full-volume IA OCR. Useful for finding pages, not for numbers.

Bonus: the same pages carry 1988 and 1986 county-by-district results.

### Other NC notes
- IA also has `northcarolinaman19931994nort`. It likely carries 1992 in the same format, which could cross-check the existing NC 1992 build.
- Not needed: NC State Archives and NCSBE historical PDFs. The Manual is the published official abstract.

## TEXAS 1990

### What the problem really is
The SRI item `micro_IA40706943_0094` (SRI accession **S7750-1**) is the TX SOS "General Election Returns/County by County Totals Report", dated Nov 27 1990. The U.S. Representative section is IA leaves **38-66**, one district per page, from District 1 on leaf 38 to District 27 on leaf 65.

- **The raw card scans do not help.** I tested the 2.3 GB "Micro Cards JP2 ZIP" (`..._micro_jp2.zip/.../0000.jp2`, the stitched card) by cropping the leaf-38 region at full resolution. It is the same 4146x3143 px as the processed page and shows the same optical softness. The blur comes from the fiche itself, not from IA's compression.
- **Each district page has a printed TOTAL row.** For example, CD1 prints 56,954 / 89,241, which exactly equals FEC for Hodges and Chapman. So county columns can be checked against that row, and against `fec1990_house.csv`, to resolve 0/6/8 misreads, as was done for the WV Blue Books. This works when a column has at most one ambiguous cell. Single-county districts need no transcription beyond the total. The remaining risk is concentrated in the large multi-county rural districts: CD 1, 2, 4, 6, 11, 13, 14, 15, 17, 19 and 21.

### Ranked leads

1. **ICPSR 13, "General Election Data for the United States, 1950-1990"**: `https://www.icpsr.umich.edu/web/ICPSR/studies/13`
   County-level returns for President, Governor, U.S. Senate **and U.S. Representative**, 1950-1990, in national files. V2 is dated 2013-11-22. Formats: ASCII, SAS, SPSS, Stata, R, tsv.
   Access: **ICPSR member institutions only.** Download needs a login from a member university (most U.S. research universities).
   Use: an independent machine-readable second reading of TX 1990 House by county, and of NC too. It could settle the ambiguous SRI digits, or replace the SRI transcription outright.
   Caveats: this is not verified yet. Check how ICPSR handles counties split between districts (county-part records versus a whole-county figure). Its 1990 figures probably derive from the same SOS canvass. The site blocks curl with Cloudflare; the details above come from an archived copy of the study page.

2. **Dave Leip's Atlas, "1990 U.S. House (Prelim)", county and CD level**: `https://uselectionatlas.org/BOTTOM/store_data.php`
   $129 individual or $515 site license. It is "Prelim" because of Pennsylvania county gaps; Texas should be complete. Its Data Sources sheet names the source used. The atlas cites "Texas Secretary of State, Elections Division, General Election Returns by County ... Official 1990" for Governor, the same SOS series.
   Licensing: republishing requires a contract with Leip, so use it only as a private cross-check for the open dataset.
   American University has a Figshare deposit, "U.S. House Election Data 1990-2024" (`https://aura.american.edu/articles/dataset/U_S_House_Election_Data_1990-2024/30200227`). It is the same Leip data, embargoed to AU users and marked In Copyright.

3. **Texas State Archives (TSLAC), "Texas Secretary of State election returns (precinct-by-precinct), 1972-2004"**
   Finding aid: `https://txarchives.org/tslac/finding_aids/30008.xml`, which is behind a bot check; archived copy at `http://web.archive.org/web/20211028070939/http://legacy.lib.utexas.edu/taro/tslac/30008/tsl-30008.html`.
   The **1990 general returns are Accession 2014/108, Boxes 2014/108-1 through 2014/108-19** (Anderson thru Shelby Counties). Examples: Box 4 is Dallas, Box 10 is Harris thru Harrison, Box 12 is Jefferson, Box 17 is Nueces. **The finding aid states that Sherman thru Zavala Counties for 1990 are missing.** The box ranges also skip some alphabetical runs, for example Knox-Leon and Medina-Mitchell. So this is a partial original-paper source: the county-certified precinct returns with county totals.
   Access: on-site only (Lorenzo de Zavala Building, Austin). Records must be requested in advance because some are at the State Records Center. Mail photocopy requests are possible for a fee: ref@tsl.texas.gov, 512-463-5455.
   Best use: spot-checking specific ambiguous SRI cells, not a full rebuild.
   The county-by-county series (finding aid 30018, 1846-1984) and "SOS election materials" parts I and II (30060 and 30106, through 1984) do **not** reach 1990.

4. **Texas Secretary of State, Elections Division.** A Texas Public Information Act request for the 1990 General Election canvass "County by County Totals Report" (U.S. Representative), the same printout SRI filmed. Whether the SOS kept a copy is unknown. Free or low cost if it exists.

5. **Other physical copies of SRI fiche S7750-1.** The Statistical Reference Index microfiche set (Congressional Information Service/ProQuest, 1991 annual) is held by many government-documents libraries, such as UT Austin PCL, Texas A&M and Rice Fondren. A careful scan on a modern fiche scanner might read better than IA's, but it is the same film generation, so gains are uncertain. I did not check WorldCat, because search.worldcat.org requires accepting OCLC's Terms of Service first. The user can search there for "Statistical reference index microfiche 1991".

### Checked and empty (Texas)
- **Texas Almanac 1992-1993** (Portal to Texas History, `https://texashistory.unt.edu/ark:/67531/metapth279642/`, pp. 426-428 of the book): it has the official canvassed 1990 results, but **congressional races only as district totals**. The county tables (pp. 429-436) cover only the primaries and the statewide Governor, Lt. Governor and U.S. Senate races. There is no county-by-district House table.
- **TX SOS online archive**: starts in 1992, as already known.
- **Texas Legislative Council / Capitol Data Portal** (`data.capitol.texas.gov`): nothing earlier than the 2000s. The Legislative Reference Library's 1990-cycle redistricting documents (`lrl.texas.gov/legis/redistricting/RedSearchResults.cfm?redCycle=1990`) contain plan and population reports, not 1990 House returns.
- **HathiTrust**: the catalog has no Texas SOS canvass volume. A full-text search for "Hamp Hodges" (a 1990 TX-1 candidate) finds only FEC Record, CQ Weekly, the ANES codebooks, America Votes 22 and Texas State Directory. None has county-level House data.
- **Portal to Texas History full-text search**: behind a bot-check page, which I did not try to get past. A web search found only an unrelated 1990 Dallas primary precinct handout (`metadc1786946`). The user may want to search the Portal manually for "County by County Totals Report" or "General Election Returns 1990".
- **uselectionatlas.org free pages**: Governor, Senate and state offices only for 1990. There is no House view.
- **America Votes 19 (1990)**: district totals only, per project notes.

### Suggested Texas path
Transcribe from the SRI leaves 38-66, forcing every column to match the printed TOTAL row, which already equals FEC. Log the cells that stay ambiguous. Then resolve them with ICPSR 13, if a member login is available, or Leip 1990 House as a private cross-check. As a last resort, request photocopies of the specific counties from TSLAC Accession 2014/108.
