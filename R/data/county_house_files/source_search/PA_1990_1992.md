# Pennsylvania U.S. House by county, 1990 and 1992: source search (2026-09-24)

Goal: November general-election returns for Representative in Congress, by county within each district, all candidates, for 1990 and 1992.

Already known before this search (not repeated below): PA DOS 1992 precinct file (local; incomplete), PA Manual 1991/1993 (no county House tables), IA SRI microfiche (nothing for PA), OpenElections (starts 2000), MEDSL (starts 2016), Clerk/FEC (district totals only).

## Key finding

The Mayda et al. replication README (`119372-V1/Readme.pdf`) names the authors' own sources:
- **House 1992 and later**: CQ Press Library, i.e. the *CQ Voting and Elections Collection*, which has House results by county ("earliest year available is 1992").
- **House before 1992, including 1990**: ICPSR 13, *General Election Data for the United States, 1950-1990*, **DS15 "National, 1990"** (file `House_1990.dta` in their package).

So both years exist as county-level data. Both are behind a paywall or institutional login.

## 1990: ranked leads

### 1. ICPSR 13, General Election Data for the United States, 1950-1990, DS15 "National, 1990" (best lead)
- URL: https://www.icpsr.umich.edu/web/ICPSR/studies/13 (doi:10.3886/ICPSR00013.v2), dataset DS15 (9 MB).
- Contents (checked on the ICPSR variables page): DS15 has a `DISTRICT` variable (V7) and party-coded congressional vote variables `990 3 G CONG <party code> VOTE` (V27-V47 and more, 44 CONG variables, for example party codes 0100 = Dem, 0200 = Rep, and minor-party codes), plus a total. Records are county units with a district number. That is county x district by party, which for 1990 is effectively by candidate. The same file has 1990 Governor by county.
- Access: free only to users at ICPSR member institutions; otherwise it has to be requested or bought from ICPSR. It is **not freely redistributable**, so it is fine for cross-checking but a licensing problem as the published source. Mayda et al. used this exact file.
- Not downloaded (needs login).

### 2. Record of American Democracy (ROAD), PA 1990 MCD-group file: free, **downloaded**, PARTIAL
- Harvard Dataverse, "Record of American Democracy, All Precincts Data Files", doi:10.7910/DVN/SGEESJ. Files `pa90mp20` (datafile id 103727, tab) and `pa90mp20.por` (id 103799, SPSS portable). Also in the per-state dataset doi:10.7910/DVN/UMTNSX. King, Palmquist, Adams, Altman, Benoit, Gay, Lewis, Mayer & Reinhardt.
- Saved: `R/data/county_house_files/pennsylvania/leads/ROAD_pa90mp20.por` (use this one) and `ROAD_pa90mp20.tab`. **The Dataverse .tab conversion is garbled** (for example ARENDTSVILLE G90G_DV = 5400 in the .tab but 180 in the .por), so read the .por with `foreign::read.spss`.
- Contents (verified): 9,507 MCD-group/precinct rows covering all 67 counties. Fields: `CY` (county FIPS), `CD` (1982-plan district), and House votes `G90H_DV`, `G90H_RV`, `G90H_TV`, meaning **Democratic and Republican only, with no minor-party candidates**. It also has Governor 1990 (G90G_*) and other offices.
- Gaps (verified): House votes are 0 or missing for **Philadelphia (101), Bucks (17), Columbia (37), Dauphin (43), Delaware (45), York (133)**. Districts 1, 2, 3 and 7 have no House votes. District 17 is at 45% of the Governor vote and district 11 at 70%. The CD coding is also off in places: district 4 House votes are 1.38x the Governor vote, and Beaver and Carbon have House votes but no Governor votes. Most other counties are within 0.85-1.02 of the Governor vote, and a ratio below 1 is expected where a seat was uncontested.
- Use: not usable alone. It could supply D/R county-by-district figures for about 55 counties, to be checked against district totals.
- Note: in 1990, districts 1-3 lie wholly inside Philadelphia (ROAD CD codes). Philadelphia's parts of those districts therefore equal the district totals. The Philadelphia parts of districts 7 and 13 are the district total minus the Delaware or Montgomery part.

### 3. Pennsylvania State Archives, RG-26 (Department of State)
- Series #26.84 "Voter Registration and Election Returns, 1971-1989", microfilm rolls 4952-4962 (https://www.phmc.state.pa.us/bah/dam/rg/rgmicro.htm). **It ends at 1989**, so 1990 would be in a later accession that is not listed online. Ask State Archives reference (ra-statearchives@pa.gov) whether the 1990/1992 certified county returns ("Official returns" or county certificates of the Bureau of Commissions, Elections and Legislation) were accessioned. Access: on-site in Harrisburg or by paid copy request.

### 4. PA Department of State, Bureau of Election Services and Notaries (the certified county returns themselves)
- DOS holds the certified returns from each county board. Its bulk-data page (https://www.pa.gov/agencies/dos/resources/voting-and-elections-resources/voting-and-election-statistics/election-data) starts with **1992 General**, and has nothing for 1990. electionreturns.pa.gov starts in 2000 (checked the archive dropdown). A Right-to-Know request to DOS for "certified county returns, Representative in Congress, November 6, 1990 (and November 3, 1992)" is the official route. The 1992 file's ReadMe gives the old contact bcel@pados.state.pa.us, which is defunct; use the current DOS RTK office.

## 1992: ranked leads

### 1. CQ Voting and Elections Collection (CQ Press / SAGE), House results by county, 1992 onward (best lead)
- https://library.cqpress.com/elections/ (now redirects to cqpresscollections.sagepub.com). Library guides describe it as having "party election data by state, congressional district, county, and town for the U.S. House of Representative Elections (1992-2024)", with the district number reported for each county. Mayda et al. used it for 1992 and later.
- Access: institutional subscription (Harvard, Princeton, Columbia, Georgetown and many state universities); export to CSV works. Candidate names and major parties are included; check whether minor-party candidates are broken out.
- Licensing: proprietary. Use it for cross-checking or with permission.

### 2. PA DOS 1992 precinct file (already local) plus targeted county patches
Diagnosis (this search compared the file's House votes with the 1992 presidential votes in the same precincts and with Clerk totals):
- **D4**: all **70 Allegheny County precincts in D4 have House votes = 0** (their presidential votes are present). That is the whole Klink/Johnston shortfall (Clerk 186,684 / 48,484 vs file 160,681 / 40,412). Beaver, Butler, Lawrence and Westmoreland look complete.
- **D10**: McDade is recorded only on the DEM line (159,469 vs Clerk 189,414). His Republican-line votes are missing in **every D10 county** (Lackawanna House/President ratio 0.58; Lycoming, Monroe, Pike and Wayne 0.86-0.88).
- **Smaller gaps**: D11 Fescina -3.6%; D13 Fox -1.9% and Margolies-Mezvinsky -1.9%; D16 Walker -2.6% and Peters -1.4%; D21 Harkins +4.1%. Clinger (D5) and Shuster (D9) are coded DEM in the file but are Republicans who also won the Democratic nomination.
- So a free fix needs Allegheny's D4 portion (Allegheny County Elections Division 1992 official returns; its website archive only goes back to 2007) and McDade's R-line votes for the 9 D10 counties. The county boards hold these; the other two routes are newspapers (lead 4) or State Archives/DOS (1990 leads 3-4).

### 3. PA State Archives / DOS Right-to-Know
Same as 1990 leads 3-4. For 1992 this is the only official route to the certified county-by-district canvass.

### 4. Newspapers (last resort)
- Note: **both Pittsburgh dailies were on strike from May 1992 to January 1993**, so there is no Post-Gazette or Pittsburgh Press coverage of November 1992. For the D4 Allegheny part, try the Valley News Dispatch (Tarentum), the McKeesport Daily News, or the Beaver County Times (Google News Archive has the Beaver County Times and the Observer-Reporter).
- For D10: Scranton Times and Scranton Tribune (Lackawanna), Williamsport Sun-Gazette, Pocono Record. Microfilm is at the Scranton Public Library, the Osterhout Library (Wilkes-Barre) and the State Library of PA newspaper collection.

## Checked, nothing usable

- **pa.gov DOS bulk data**: earliest general-election file is 1992 (the online copy is byte-identical to the local file, 38,953,995 bytes). No 1990 file.
- **electionreturns.pa.gov**: archive starts at the 2000 General.
- **Wayback captures of dos.state.pa.us**: `bcel/arc/genelec_1960_2000.html` (2001-02 captures) holds statewide turnout only; `elec_arc.html`, `98genreturns.htm` and the other `bcel/arc/*` pages cover 1998 and later only.
- **Wilkes University Election Statistics Project** (Harold Cox, staffweb.wilkes.edu/harold.cox/): county returns for President, Senate and Governor only. The House section has district-level PDFs posted only for 1788-1916.
- **Penn Libraries election guide**: points to the Wilkes project and to the Allegheny College "Pennsylvania Counties Archive" (county turnout only; the site is behind Cloudflare and was blocked).
- **Dave Leip store**: U.S. House county data starts in 2000.
- **HathiTrust full-text** (McDade/Kanjorski/county names/"Representative in Congress"): only legislative directories, hearings and similar. The catalog subject search "Elections -- Pennsylvania -- Statistics" found nothing for the 1990s. The Philadelphia "Annual report of the City Commissioners" shows only early (about 1900-1905) HathiTrust records in the subject search.
- **Harvard Dataverse** searches (county congressional 1992, PA precinct 1990 and similar): only MEDSL constituency-level data and 2016+ precinct data.
- **Allegheny County Elections**: online results archive covers 2007-2021. Pitt's Historic Pittsburgh "Election Returns of Allegheny County, 1926-1983" (AIS.1965.10) ends in 1983.
- **Philadelphia City Commissioners** (vote.phila.gov historical and archived data sets): only 2000s data sets online.
- **Internet Archive** metadata search for PA election returns: only 19th-century election law pamphlets. IA was intermittently offline during the search.
- **Google Books API**: daily quota exhausted, not checked.
- **WorldCat**: requires accepting OCLC terms in the browser, which was not done. Not searched directly, and web search for WorldCat records found nothing relevant. A WorldCat check for a DOS "official returns" series held by the State Library of PA or Penn State is still open.
- **ICPSR search** for a 1992 county-level House study: no 1992 successor to ICPSR 13.
