# Source search: MS 1996, AR 2004, DE 1998 U.S. House county results

Searched 2026-09-24. Files downloaded to `R/data/county_house_files/<state>/leads/`.

## ARKANSAS 2004: FOUND (official, born-digital, verified)

**Lead 1 (use this one).** Arkansas Secretary of State, *2004 General Election and Non-Partisan Judicial Runoff Certification Report* (11/02/2004).
- URL: https://www.sos.arkansas.gov/uploads/elections/2004_General_Election_and_Non-Partisan_Judicial_Runoff_Certification_Report.pdf
  (the same file is also at https://ee-sos-site.ark.org/uploads/elections/...; Wayback has a broken capture of a garbled path)
- Local: `arkansas/leads/AR_2004_General_Election_Certification_Report.pdf` (1,663 pages, 19.8 MB, text layer produced by Acrobat Web Capture, so no OCR is needed)
- Contents: Summary report, then County Reports, then Detailed (precinct) Reports. The county blocks are titled `U.S. Congress District 0N (<County> County)` and list each candidate's votes, plus over and under votes.
- Checked: parsing the county blocks with `pdftotext -layout` gives 46 counties (D1 = 26, D2 = 8, D3 = 12). In every district the county sums equal the statewide certified totals exactly:
  - D1: Berry (D) 162,388; Humphrey (R) 81,556
  - D2: Snyder (D) 160,834; Parks (R) 115,655; Gabriel (write-in) 4
  - D3: Boozman (R) 160,629; Judy (D) 103,158; Morfey (I) 7,016

  Each county appears twice in the text (County Reports and Detailed Reports), and the two copies agree.
- **District 4:** Mike Ross (D) was unopposed. The report says it covers "Vote totals for all opposed candidates", so D4's 29 counties have no vote counts. FEC also lists Ross as unopposed. Treat D4 as unopposed with no votes, as the project does for other unopposed races.
- Also checked: OpenElections `openelections-data-ar` has no 2004 folder (it has 2002, then 2006 onward). `openelections-sources-ar` has nothing for 2004.

## DELAWARE 1998: FOUND (official results by election district; county totals derived and validated)

**Lead 1.** Delaware Department of Elections, *1998 General Election – Results By District* (dated 12/02/98, 400 of 400 districts).
- Current URL (the live site returns "Request Rejected" to curl): https://elections.delaware.gov/elections/resultsarchive/elect98/elect98_general/elect98_general_edrd.shtml
- Wayback capture used: https://web.archive.org/web/20231230143342id_/https://elections.delaware.gov/elections/resultsarchive/elect98/elect98_general/elect98_general_edrd.shtml
- Local: `delaware/leads/DE_1998_general_results_by_ED_RD.html`, plus the statewide page `DE_1998_general_results_by_office.html`
- Contents: a "US REPRESENTATIVE" table for each of the 41 representative districts, broken down by election district (ED id like `01-28`), with an RD TOT row. Four candidates: Williams (D), Castle (R), Bemis (Natural Law), Webster (U.S. Taxpayers).
- The official source has **no county breakdown**. The earlier 1990-96 years had one, in scanned PDFs at `elect9x/pdfs/`. For 1998, CDX shows no PDF.
- **County totals derived here.** I mapped each ED to a county using OpenElections' `election_districts_1992-2002.csv` (https://github.com/openelections/openelections-data-de). All 400 EDs from 1998 are in that map.
  - The ED sum equals the statewide total exactly: 57,446 / 119,811 / 859 / 2,411.
  - Every ED row's own TOTAL equals its candidate sum.
  - **Mapping check:** I applied the same map to the 2000 general, which uses the same 1992-2002 district plan. The 2000 ED file is `elect00/2000gened.shtml` and the official county page is `elect00/2000gencounty.shtml`. The derived Kent, Sussex and New Castle (Wilmington + rest of the county) totals for U.S. House match the official 2000 county page exactly for all candidates.
  - Only RD 28 (ED 01-28 in New Castle, 02-07 in Kent) and RD 35 (01-03 in Kent, 04-09 in Sussex) cross county lines. Their ED lists are the same in 1998 and 2000.
- Result: `delaware/leads/DE_1998_house_county_aggregated_from_ED.csv`

  | County | Williams (D) | Castle (R) | Bemis (NL) | Webster (UST) |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | New Castle | 38,460 | 72,439 | 559 | 1,483 |
  | Kent | 7,985 | 19,333 | 126 | 446 |
  | Sussex | 11,001 | 28,039 | 174 | 482 |

- Validation files are kept in `delaware/leads/`: `DE_2000_general_by_ED_validation.html` and `DE_2000_general_by_county_validation.html`.
- Also checked: OpenElections `openelections-data-de` has no 1998 folder (it starts in 2000). There is no 1998 PDF in the resultsarchive.

## MISSISSIPPI 1996: no official county file online; the best source is offline at MDAH. There is a partial D/R-only precinct dataset.

**Lead 1 (official, offline): Mississippi Department of Archives and History (MDAH), Series 107 "Election Returns" (Secretary of State, RG 28).**
- Finding aid: https://finding.mdah.ms.gov/government-records/series-107 (no access restrictions; 494 cu. ft.)
- Relevant boxes:
  - **"1996: general election, certified results" — Box 20366**
  - **"1996: general election spreadsheet summary" — Box 20366**
  - "1996: general election, summary" — Box 19428
  - The county return files for the 1996 general are in Boxes 19428-19433.
- MDAH, 200 North St., Jackson MS; reference 601-576-6850. Ask for scans or copies of Box 20366 by email or through a reference request. Box 20366 very likely holds the SOS certified recap by county for President, Senate and the 5 House districts. This is the same kind of document that came from SRI for 1994.

**Lead 2 (official book, print only): *Mississippi Official and Statistical Register 1996-2000* (Blue Book).**
- By analogy with the other editions, this edition should hold the 1994 and 1996 county results. The 1992-96 edition covered 1990/92 and the 2000-04 edition covers 1998/2000.
- HathiTrust holds it search-only: `umn.31951d01710800w` (University of Minnesota copy), https://babel.hathitrust.org/cgi/pt?id=umn.31951d01710800w. Record https://catalog.hathitrust.org/Record/000549439, OCLC 1243227, LCCN 10033135, ISSN 0196-4755.
  - I could not confirm the page contents: HathiTrust sits behind Cloudflare, and the in-volume search did not render in the browser. A person could run a full-text search for "Wicker" or "Pickering" in a browser to find the page numbers.
- Libraries: University of Minnesota (the source of the HathiTrust copy), MDAH Library, Mississippi State Law Library, Ole Miss and USM libraries (Mississippi collections). Stanford (JK4630 .A5, SAL3) holds only up to 1992-1996. Worth checking WorldCat for OCLC 1243227 holdings and an interlibrary-loan scan of the elections chapter.
- Not online: the SOS web Blue Book archive (`sos.state.ms.us/ed_pubs/BlueBook/`) only ever had the 2000-2004 and 2004-2008 editions. Internet Archive has 2000-04 onward plus 1980-84, 84-88, 88-92 and 92-96 on SRI microfiche, but not 1996-2000. eGrove (Ole Miss) has only 1929-1949.

**Lead 3 (downloaded, partial, not official tabulation): David Lublin / MS SOS Elections Division, "Precinct Data from Mississippi General Elections, 1991-1992, 1994-1996" (funded by NSF grant 9710058), hosted by MDAH Digital Archives.**
- https://da.mdah.ms.gov/series/lublin/detail/522020 (PDF: https://da.mdah.ms.gov/series-files/lublin/pdf/MS1996.pdf), README https://da.mdah.ms.gov/series-files/lublin/pdf/README96.pdf
- Local: `mississippi/leads/MDAH_Lublin_MS1996_precinct.pdf`, `MDAH_Lublin_README96.pdf`, and my parse `MDAH_Lublin_MS1996_precinct_parsed.csv` (2,129 precinct rows; columns placed by x-position because the layout differs from page to page).
- Contents: precinct-level President (D/R/Reform), Senate (D/R), U.S. House **Democratic and Republican candidates only**, and the CD number. Minor-party and independent House candidates are missing.
- Gaps:
  - **Jackson and Perry counties (both CD5) are missing entirely**, so 80 of 82 counties are covered.
  - Totals fall short of FEC, probably because absentee votes and some precincts are missing:
    - CD1 D 55,225 vs 55,998 and R 116,265 vs 123,724
    - CD2 99,673 vs 102,503 and 62,702 vs 65,263
    - CD3 68,219 vs 68,658 and 114,319 vs 115,443
    - CD4 66,832 vs 66,836 and 112,381 vs 112,444
    - CD5 77,116 vs 103,415 (Jackson and Perry missing)
  - Three rows have a garbled CD value ("1,2" or "1.2"): Montgomery North Winona, Montgomery Lodi and Panola Curtis. They need hand checking.
- 11 counties are split between districts: Attala, Grenada, Hinds, Jones, Leake, Madison, Montgomery, Oktibbeha, Panola, Tallahatchie and Wayne. This is useful for the county-to-district split structure, and as a fallback for D/R shares, but not as the official county file.
- The same series has 1992 and 1994 files (MS1992.pdf, MS1994.pdf). These could cross-check the existing 1990/92 Register values with their +4,000 flags, and the 1994 SRI transcription.

**Came up empty for MS 1996:**
- OpenElections `openelections-data-ms` and `-sources-ms` start in 2003.
- The old SOS elections site (`sos.state.ms.us/elections/`) has recaps only for 2000 (`2000ReCap/CertResultsScans/ushouseN.pdf`), 2003 and 2005; there is nothing for 1996.
- The SRI microfiche on Internet Archive ends with the 1996 filming year. The only Mississippi election item is the 1994 summary, and there is no 1997 reel.
- Web searches found no county-by-district tables.

## Summary ranking
1. AR 2004: SOS certification PDF. Complete for the three contested districts, ties exactly to certified totals. D4 unopposed.
2. DE 1998: official ED-level page aggregated to counties. Ties exactly statewide, and the map is validated against official 2000 county results.
3. MS 1996:
   - MDAH Series 107 Box 20366 (certified results and spreadsheet summary): best, needs a request to MDAH.
   - 1996-2000 Register (print or HathiTrust search-only): next.
   - Lublin precinct data (online, D/R only, 80/82 counties, short of certified totals): fallback.
