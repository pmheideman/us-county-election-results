# Source search: Ohio 1998 and Michigan 1996, U.S. House by county within district

Searched 2026-09-24. Nothing downloadable online has the county-by-district U.S. House table for
either state and year. Both tables exist in print (and on SRI microfiche), and the physical copies are
listed below. No files were downloaded: the only candidate file, the Ohio SOS 1998 xlsx, turned out to
have district totals only.

---

## OHIO 1998 (88 counties, 19 districts)

### Ranked leads

1. **Ohio Election Statistics 1997-1998** (Ohio Secretary of State, biennial). This volume has
   "District Vote for Representative to Congress" by county. It is **not digitized** anywhere I could find.
   - HathiTrust has the series under three records: catalog.hathitrust.org/Record/000546832 (OCLC 5470967,
     LC JK5593), Record/101672181 (OCLC 2427747, law call number KFO420.6.A152 O45) and Record/008637098.
     All three are search-only for the modern volumes. The Ohio State University copies scanned by Google
     are **1993-94 (osu.32435076257351), 1995-96 (osu.32435060266871 and osu.32437011276926), 2001-02,
     2003-04 and 2007-08**. No 1997-98 or 1999-2000 volume is digitized under any record, which suggests
     OSU's copies of those two volumes were never scanned. The series ran through at least 2008, so a
     1997-98 volume almost certainly exists.
   - Physical copies to request: **The Ohio State University Libraries** (the source of the HathiTrust
     copies; LC JK5593, and the Moritz Law Library copy is KFO420.6.A152 O45); **State Library of Ohio**,
     Columbus (Ohio documents depository); **Ohio History Connection** archives/library. Other Ohio
     depository libraries can be found by searching WorldCat for OCLC 5470967 or 2427747. I did not
     complete the WorldCat search because it requires accepting OCLC's terms of service.
   - **SRI microfiche**: this series is in the Statistical Reference Index as **accession S6380-1**. The
     1993-94 volume is on the Internet Archive as micro_IA40706953_0173 (catalog_number S6380-1). The
     1997-98 volume would be on the **1999 SRI fiche**, held at any library with a ProQuest/CIS SRI
     microfiche subscription (most large university government-documents departments).
   - **Watch the Internet Archive.** The SRI microfiche collection (collection:statistical-reference-index)
     is being scanned right now. Items are dated scandate 2026-09-04 to 2026-09-22. The highest box so far
     is IA40706954, which holds the 1996 fiche. Boxes IA40706955 and later do not exist yet. If the uploads
     continue into the 1997-1999 fiche, both "Ohio Election Statistics 1997-98" (S6380-1) and Michigan's
     1996 results (S4020-1, see below) could appear. To check, search
     `archive.org/advancedsearch.php?q=collection:(statistical-reference-index) AND title:(election)`
     sorted by addeddate.

2. **County boards of elections**, as a partial fallback that covers split counties only. Some BOEs
   post pre-2000 results, for example Lorain: voteloraincountyohio.gov has a "1998 General Election
   Information" page. Assembling all 88 counties this way is impractical, but it could fill in split
   counties (Cuyahoga, Franklin, Hamilton, Summit and others) if another source only gives some of them.

### Came up empty (Ohio)
- **Ohio SOS Data Portal** (data.ohiosos.gov/portal/past-election-results). The file index is at
  publicfiles.ohiosos.gov/election-results/files-index.json.
  `past-elections/1998/General Election: November 3, 1998/1998_us_house_of_representatives_november_3.xlsx`
  was fetched and parsed in the browser. It has **district TOTAL rows only** (57 rows, first column only
  "TOTAL" or "Ward/Precinct Name"), with the same numbers as the archived USreps110398.aspx page. The
  portal has no PDFs before 2003. Plain curl gets a Cloudflare "maintenance" page (403); only a browser
  can fetch the files.
- **Old SOS sites in the Wayback Machine** (www.state.oh.us/sos/, sos.state.oh.us, www.sos.state.oh.us):
  - `1998_congress_general_election_r.htm` (2000 capture) has district totals only, and they are
    unofficial (e.g. CD1 90,060/79,016 against the official 92,421/82,003).
  - `/sos/98gen-uscongress.pdf` (a 2003 URL that redirects to www.sos.state.oh.us/sos/98gen-uscongress.pdf)
    was **never captured**.
  - `results/1998/98general.htm` links only to the same district-level pages.
  - The later `electResultsMain/1990-1999OfficialElectionResults/USreps110398.aspx` has district totals only.
- **SRI microfiche on the Internet Archive**: the Ohio items end with 1993-94 (see lead 1). The untitled
  1996 items in box IA40706954 were checked through their OCR text and are trade-association reports, not
  state election documents.
- **OpenElections**: openelections-data-oh starts at 2000, and openelections-sources-oh has only 1992
  and 1996 files (Clark and Montgomery counties).
- **CNN AllPolitics 1998** (cnn.com/ELECTION/1998/states/OH/H/NN/, archived): district totals and
  precinct counts only, no county breakdown.
- **HathiTrust full-text search** on 1998 candidate names combined with county names found only CQ,
  Almanac and Yellow Book titles. None had county-level tables.

## MICHIGAN 1996 (83 counties, 16 districts)

### Ranked leads

1. **Michigan Manual 1997-1998** (Legislative Service Bureau). Chapter "Elections", "Official Canvass
   of Votes, General Election 1996: U.S. House of Representatives", has the same county-by-district
   layout as the 1991-92 and 1993-94 editions already used for 1990 and 1992.
   - **HathiTrust mdp.39015071179413** (University of Michigan copy, Google scan) is **search-only**:
     rights "ic", in copyright to the Legislative Council. A full-text search confirms it contains the
     1996 House candidates together with county names (Stabenow, Chrysler, Stupak, Kilpatrick, Ingham,
     Shiawassee, Alcona, Keweenaw). The record is catalog.hathitrust.org/Record/000060510, OCLC 1643917,
     ISSN 0091-1933, **LC call number JK5830 .A32**. HathiTrust's page-level search viewer did not render
     for me, so I could not get exact page numbers. By analogy with the 1999-2000 edition, where the 1998
     canvass starts at p. 829 in Ch. IX, the 1996 House general canvass should be around pp. 830-880 of
     the 1997-98 edition. This is an estimate.
   - **Michigan Legislature site** (legislature.mi.gov/Legislature/MichiganManuals?sessionID=2): the
     1997-1998 edition is online **only for Ch. III (Legislative Branch, pp. 123-357)**. The page itself
     says "A subset of pages is available for Michigan Manuals from before 1999". Probing guessed filenames
     like `documents/1997-1998/michiganmanual/1997-MM-P0800-P0800.pdf` returns 404. Full editions start
     with 1999-2000.
   - **Physical copies**: University of Michigan Hatcher Graduate Library (JK5830 .A32; the HathiTrust
     scan is from this copy); **Library of Michigan**, Lansing (state documents, the most complete run);
     Michigan State University Libraries (catalog record b2215559, per the MSU "Michigan Manual" LibGuide);
     plus most Michigan public and academic libraries. The Manual was widely distributed.
   - The Internet Archive lending library has the 1991-92 (michiganmanual190000unse), 1993-94
     (michiganmanual00mich) and 2001-02 (michiganmanual200000unse) editions, but **not 1997-98**.

2. **Michigan Bureau of Elections, "State of Michigan General Election, Nov. 5, 1996" (official
   results)**, in SRI as **accession S4020-1**. The 1994 edition is micro_IA40706952_0453, which the
   project already used for 1994. The 1996 edition would be on the **1997 SRI fiche**, which is **not yet
   on the Internet Archive**; see the upload note under Ohio lead 1. It is available on SRI microfiche at
   SRI-subscribing libraries now. The printed original should also be at the Library of Michigan and the
   Archives of Michigan (michigan.gov/mhc/archives). The SOS results page says "Historical election data
   is available through the State of Michigan Archives."

### Came up empty (Michigan)
- **Michigan SOS electronic results** start with 1998 in every form:
  - miboecfr.nictusa.com and miboecfr.nicusa.com `/election/results/`: about 13k Wayback URLs, earliest
    folder 98GEN/98PRI; the DATA/ xls files start at 1998GEN.
  - sos.state.mi.us `/election/results/`: 98gen and 00gen only.
  - michigan.gov/sos "Election results and data" table: the earliest entry is "1998 - current".
  - mielections.us: no response.
- **OpenElections MI**: data and sources both start at 1998.
- **SRI microfiche on the Internet Archive**: the Michigan general-election items run 1980-1994 only.
- **HathiTrust "Official canvass of votes" (Michigan Dept. of State) records**: no volumes after the 1970s.
- **Grace York's U-M Michigan elections page** (archived): points to the Manual and the SOS 98gen+
  pages. Nothing for 1996 at county level.

## Recommended next step
Both targets need a library request or a visit. Asking for scans of specific pages is the quickest route:
- **MI 1996**: the U-M Hatcher Documents Center or the Library of Michigan scans the "U.S. House of
  Representatives, General Election 1996" pages of the Michigan Manual 1997-1998 (JK5830 .A32). A
  HathiTrust accessibility or rights-holder request is another option, since the Legislative Council
  holds the copyright.
- **OH 1998**: the OSU Libraries or the State Library of Ohio scans the "Representative to Congress"
  section of Ohio Election Statistics 1997-1998 (JK5593 / KFO420.6.A152 O45). The alternative is the
  1999 SRI microfiche, S6380-1.
- Recheck the Internet Archive SRI collection for boxes after IA40706954 in a few weeks.
