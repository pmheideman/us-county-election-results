# Washington U.S. House by county, 1990-1998: source search

Searched 2026-09-24. **Result: solved for all five years.** The data comes from a free official online source, and every district total checks out.

## Lead 1 (best, all years): WA Secretary of State "Election Search" database, county breakdown pages

The SOS still hosts its old ASP.NET historical results database, which covers 1900-2006. It is separate from the current "Election Results Archive" xlsx, which has only district totals. Every race in it has a **"Show County Breakdown"** link. The link opens a modal page that lists each county's votes by candidate, with party labels, and includes every ballot-listed candidate.

- Race page (district totals, Federal offices):
  `https://www.sos.wa.gov/elections/results_report.aspx?e=<E>&c=&c2=&t=&t2=1&p=&p2=&y=`
- County breakdown:
  `https://www.sos.wa.gov/elections/results_report_county_breakdown.aspx?e=<E>&t=<T>`
- Election IDs (E): 1990 General = 24, 1992 General = 3, 1994 General = 15, 1996 General = 17, 1998 General = 10
  (from the ElectionID dropdown on `https://www.sos.wa.gov/elections/results_search.aspx`)
- Office IDs (T), "U. S. Representative District #N": CD1 = 857, CD2 = 861, CD3 = 864, CD4 = 867, CD5 = 868, CD6 = 869, CD7 = 863, CD8 = 865, CD9 = 872
  (also: 866 = CD3 Unexpired, 1007 = Congressman-At-Large, 985 = U.S. Senator)
- Access: free. The pages were live on 2026-09-24 and plain `curl` works. The site sits behind Cloudflare, and a burst of about 5 requests in a row got a "Page not found" page (about 184 KB) instead of data. Waiting 5 seconds and retrying fixed it.
- Backup copies: the Wayback Machine has one capture of every one of these 44 county-breakdown URLs. It holds about 7,200 archived `results_report_county_breakdown.aspx` pages in total, for example `https://web.archive.org/web/20160122173115/http://www.sos.wa.gov/elections/results_report_county_breakdown.aspx?e=10&t=857`.

### Downloaded and verified
Files are in `R/data/county_house_files/washington/leads/sos_county_breakdown/`:
- `wa_<year>_cd<N>_e<E>_t<T>.html`: 44 county-breakdown pages. There are 8 districts in 1990, since WA had 8 seats before the 1992 reapportionment; the CD9 query for 1990 returns "County breakdown unavailable" and was dropped. There are 9 districts in each of 1992-1998.
- `wa_<year>_federal_statewide_e<E>.html`: the district-total race pages for each year.
- `wa_house_county_1990_1998_sos_db.csv`: parsed long table (year, district, county, candidate, party, votes), 602 rows.

Checks:
- All 39 counties appear in every year. The split counties are listed under each district they fall in (e.g. 1990: King in CD 1/2/6/7/8, Pierce in 3/6/8; 1998: King in 1/7/8/9, Pierce in 6/8/9, Thurston in 3/9, Adams in 4/5, Klickitat in 3/4).
- For every candidate in every district and year, the county sums **exactly equal** the database's district totals.
- Those totals **exactly equal** the official figures: FEC 1990 (`R/data/fec_official/fec1990_house.csv`) and the Clerk of the House Statistics for 1992, 1994, 1996 and 1998 (`R/data/clerk_house_stats/*Stat.htm`). All 44 contests match, for every candidate.
- No write-in lines appear. The Clerk's WA figures also show no write-ins.

Caveats:
- **Party labels in the SOS database are sometimes wrong.** In 1998 CD4, Gordon Allen Pross is coded "R", but the Clerk says Democrat. In 1998 CD7, Stan Lippmann is coded "L", but the Clerk says Reform. Other codes: IC = Independent Candidate, NL/NLP = Natural Law, AH = American Heritage, RFM = Reform, GC = Gun Control, SW = Socialist Workers. Party labels should come from the Clerk, or from `clerk_house_party_lookup.csv`.
- Name spellings differ slightly from the Clerk: "Taber Taber" is Ron Taber (1998 CD9), and "Judy  Olson" has a double space.
- The page footer says 1900-2006 are "receiving a final proof" (flagged as "State Totals Proofed" or "County Totals Proofed"). None of these five elections shows a proof flag, so the county figures are not marked as proofed. Even so, they reconcile exactly to the certified district totals.
- This is a SOS compilation from the county canvasses, not a scan of the printed abstract. That is the same kind of source as the 2008/2010 SOS downloads the project already uses.

## Secondary / fallback leads (not needed given Lead 1)
2. **Wayback Machine captures of the same pages.** Use the CDX query `https://web.archive.org/cdx/search/cdx?url=sos.wa.gov/elections/results_report_county_breakdown.aspx&matchType=prefix`. It has captures from 2016 onward and is useful if the SOS takes the old app offline.
3. **Printed official abstracts** ("Abstract of Votes" / official canvass). These were not pursued once Lead 1 was verified. The SOS Elections Division (Olympia) and the Washington State Archives, Olympia (Secretary of State Elections Division records), are the custodians. The WA State Library (Tumwater) holds the state-publication series. Use these only if a cell-level audit against paper is wanted.
4. **County auditors' offices.** The SOS says pre-2007 local results come from the counties. These are unnecessary for House contests now.

## Checked and empty / insufficient
- The current SOS "Washington State Election Results Archive" xlsx has statewide and district totals only.
- The IA Statistical Reference Index microfiche has no WA general-election returns for 1990-98 (known beforehand).
- OpenElections WA starts in 2000, MEDSL starts in 2016, and the FEC and Clerk give district totals only.
- A general web search for a WA "abstract of votes" 1990s PDF found no digitized SOS abstract, only Oregon and North Carolina equivalents.
