# New Hampshire U.S. House general election by town: 1994, 1998, 2002, 2004, 2008

Search date: 2026-09-24. **Result: all five years found.** Every one comes from the official
*State of New Hampshire Manual for the General Court* (NH Dept. of State, published every odd year).
The edition after each election prints "U.S. HOUSE: FIRST DISTRICT" and "U.S. HOUSE: SECOND DISTRICT"
as full town/ward tables with a Totals row, in the "General Election" chapter. The tables are by town
only, with no county column, so we need our own town-to-county crosswalk. OpenElections ships one:
`oe_nh/mappings/town_to_county.py`.

The page extracts are in `R/data/county_house_files/new_hampshire/leads/`.

## How the tables look and what to watch for
- The PDFs are image scans. Their text layers (IA OCR) are partial or garbled; headers and names get dropped.
  Plan on **transcribing from the page images** and checking each candidate's town sum against the printed Totals
  row, the same method as for the 1996 NH build.
- Each table lists the printed candidates only. Scattered write-ins are not shown, so a total built from these
  columns will leave out write-ins. The same was true of the SOS files used for 1996/2000/2006/2010.
- Each district ends with a "No votes cast:" footnote listing unincorporated places (grants, purchases, locations)
  that recorded zero votes. Rows for Hale's Location, Pinkham's Grant, Wentworth's Location and similar do appear
  when they had votes; the crosswalk must cover them.
- City wards show up as "Manchester Ward 1", "Dover Wd 1", "Nashua Ward 1" and so on. Roll them up to the city,
  then to the county.
- Page numbering: the "IAleaf" numbers in the file names are PDF page indexes. The "pp" numbers are the printed
  book pages.

## Extra finds (cross-checks for years already built)
The same Manual series covers every even year:
- 1997 Manual `manualforgeneral55newh` covers 1996.
- 2001 Manual `manualforgeneral57newh` covers 2000 (House tables near book p. 351).
- 2007 Manual `manualforgeneral60newh` covers 2006 (House tables near book p. 269).
- 2011 Manual `manualforgeneral2011newh` (and UNH article=1061) covers 2010.
- 1991 and 1993 Manuals cover 1990 and 1992: `manualforgeneral52newh` / `micro_IA40706939_0148` and
  `manualforgeneral53newh` / `micro_IA40706946_0051`. These may fill **1990 and 1992** too, which were listed as
  gaps because the SOS site has nothing before 1996. Not checked page by page.

Each Manual also has a "COUNTY SUMMARY" / "SUMMARY BY COUNTIES" for Governor and U.S. Senate. Those give a check
on turnout but have no House breakdown.
