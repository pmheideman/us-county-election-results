# Data license

The compiled election-results data in this project (the released CSV/Parquet files, the corrections log and the coverage
tables) are licensed under the **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license (CC BY 4.0)**:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode

You may share and adapt the data for any purpose, including commercially, as long as you give appropriate credit
(cite this project and its sources, listed in `SOURCES.csv` and `docs/DECISIONS.md`) and indicate if changes were made.

Underlying results come from public records and openly licensed datasets (notably MEDSL and Algara & Amlani, both CC0, plus
state election offices and OpenElections). Our license applies to our compilation, corrections and documentation. Vote
counts themselves are factual public records, but **each upstream source keeps its own terms**, and this license cannot
override them. A few state election offices publish their results under site terms that restrict copying or reuse (at the
last check: Florida, Indiana, Colorado and Wisconsin; North Carolina and Utah allow non-commercial use with citation). The
`license` and `license_status` columns of `SOURCES.csv` record what each publisher's terms say for every source token. If you
redistribute rows from those states, check the publisher's terms for your use.
